How to play

  1. Select New game on the home page.
  2. Copy the join link and share it with the other player.
  3. Wait for the other player to open the link. The game will start automatically.
  4. When it is your turn, choose a square on the opponent's board to fire.
  5. Players alternate turns. X marks a hit and o marks a miss.
  6. Sink every opponent ship to win.

Fleet

Each player has one Carrier (5 cells), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), and Destroyer (2). Ships are placed randomly.
